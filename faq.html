FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Have questions? We have answers. Here you'll find clear, concise answers about how we work, who we work with, and what to expect when you partner with StellAcu. If you need more, the contact page is always open.

01 · What services do you offer?

We offer six integrated solutions, designed to work as a single growth architecture: Business Advisory, Inbound, Outbound, Media & Growth, AI & Automation, and Carbon-Smart Solutions. Every marketing service you'd otherwise hire five vendors for, under one roof. Whether you engage one or all six, every solution is anchored to your business outcome, not the channel.

02 · Do you only push services I don't need, or do you actually curate?

We curate. We won't push every solution on every brand. The Growth Audit tells us what you need today, what can wait, and what's not the right fit at all. We deliver what works for your business. Nothing else.

03 · Do you work with businesses of all sizes?

Yes. Early-stage startups, scale-ups, and established enterprise brands. Regardless of revenue, industry, or stage. The work scales with you. What stays constant is the methodology and the quality of thinking we bring.

04 · Where are you based, and who do you work with?

Headquartered in Bangalore, India. We work with brands globally, across Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and North America. Work happens remote-first. Thinking happens face-to-face whenever it should.

05 · What does "MarTech-led" actually mean?

It means the technology stack underneath your marketing is treated as part of the strategy, not an afterthought. We design the data layer, the tooling, and the workflows alongside the brand and the campaigns. In 2026, the gap between a great strategy and a working strategy is usually plumbing. We fix the plumbing too.

06 · How is StellAcu different from a typical marketing agency?

A typical agency sells you the activity. We sell you the answer the activity is supposed to produce. We diagnose before we prescribe, run channels we can tie to revenue, and treat your time, capital, and objectives as one balance sheet. Every dollar is a marketing dollar.

07 · What's the Growth Audit, and do I need one?

It's our five-layer diagnostic: Market Diagnosis, Funnel Mapping, Channel Alignment, Conversion Analysis, and Revenue Model Clarity. Most engagements start here because the playbook we'd write without it is a guess. With it, it's a plan you can execute on.

08 · Can I hire you for just one solution, or do I need all six?

Both. Each solution stands on its own. Together, they form one operating system. The Growth Audit tells you exactly which solutions you need today, and which ones can wait.

09 · How long does a typical engagement take?

It depends on what we're solving. Short diagnostic work typically wraps in 4 to 8 weeks. Strategy and execution engagements run 8 to 16 weeks. Retainers and integrated programs work as long as you need them. We scope and price after the first conversation, so you know exactly what you're committing to.

10 · How do you measure success?

On the metric you'd defend in a board meeting. Pipeline, revenue, qualified demand, retention, brand recall. Whichever metric your business is built on. We don't measure what's easy. We measure what matters.

11 · Can you work alongside our existing agency or in-house team?

Yes, often. We're built to integrate, not replace. We'll work with your existing partners, your in-house team, or both. If the right answer is to consolidate, we'll say that too.

12 · How do we get started?

Send a note via the contact page. We respond within 24 hours. If it sounds like a fit, we book a 30-minute scoping call. If it doesn't, we'll point you toward someone who is. The first conversation is free.
