StellAcu Media respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

StellAcu Media is a growth, marketing, and advertising firm registered in India. You can reach us at sales@stellacumedia.com.

What we collect

When you fill out a form, subscribe to our newsletter, request a Growth Audit, or contact us, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Business email and phone number
  • Company name
  • Anything you choose to tell us about what you are trying to solve

When you visit our website, we automatically collect technical information like your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, and how you got to us. This happens through cookies and standard analytics tools.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and deliver our services
  • Send you newsletters, updates, and the occasional relevant marketing email (you can unsubscribe any time)
  • Improve our website and content
  • Keep our systems secure
  • Meet our legal obligations

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the business (hosting, CRM, email tools, analytics, advertising platforms)
  • Professional advisors like our lawyers or auditors when needed
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

Anyone we share information with is required to keep it confidential.

Cookies

We use cookies to make the website work, to understand how it is used, and to measure our marketing. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies through the banner on our site, or block them through your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to do what we said we would do, or as long as the law requires. After that, we delete it or anonymise it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop using your information for marketing
  • Withdraw any consent you previously gave

Email sales@stellacumedia.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will get back to you within a reasonable time.

Data security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our website is not for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
